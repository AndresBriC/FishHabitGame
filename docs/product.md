# Product vision

## Fish Habit Game

Fish Habit Game is a calm, encouraging habit tracker with a fish-collection loop. Completing real-life habits should create gentle progress in a cozy game; missed days slow progress rather than punish the player.

## Target experience

- Open the app daily and understand what is available immediately.
- Complete habits with minimal friction.
- Discover and catch fish from a personal daily pond.
- Build a persistent collection over time.

## Core loop

1. A new daily pond becomes available.
2. The player completes habits.
3. Habit progress grants or improves fishing opportunities.
4. The player catches fish and grows a collection.

## Product principles

- Gentle motivation over punishment.
- Small daily interactions over grinding.
- The collection must feel persistent and rewarding.
- Rules must be clear enough that players trust outcomes.

## Non-goals for the first release

- Discord integration.
- Social, competitive, or multiplayer features.
- Native mobile apps or app-store distribution.
- A real-time fishing minigame.
